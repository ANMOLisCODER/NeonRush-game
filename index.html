HOW TO PLAY

🖥️ Desktop

  • ←/A · →/D — switch lane
  • ↑/W/Space — jump
  • ↓/S — slide
  • P/Esc — pause · R — restart · F3 — debug

📱 Mobile

  • Swipe left / right — switch lane
  • Swipe up or tap — jump
  • Swipe down — slide
  • ❚❚ button — pause

🎯 Goal

  • Dodge barriers, signs and metro trains
  • Collect ⬡ tokens and power-ups
  • Clean running raises your multiplier up to x5
  • You have 3 lives — shields absorb one hit

⚡ Power-ups

  • Magnet — attracts nearby tokens
  • Shield — absorbs one collision
  • x2 Score — double points
  • Jet Boost — fly over everything

CREDITS

Neon Metro Rush — an original 3D endless runner built as a Computer Graphics major project.

Design · Code · 3D · Procedural Art · Audio Synthesis
Solo student project, 2025–26

Engine & libraries
Three.js (MIT) · TypeScript (Apache-2.0) · Vite (MIT) · Vitest (MIT)

Assets
100% procedural — all textures, models, characters and sounds are generated at runtime by original code. No third-party art or audio is used. See ASSET_CREDITS.md.

Inspiration
The lane-based endless-runner genre and the neon night aesthetic of Indian metro cities. All names, signs, characters and designs are fictional and original.
